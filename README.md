Solmar Heat is a 3D open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Roam a living 9×9-block city on foot or behind the wheel, weave through traffic, steal cars, dodge or outrun the police as your wanted level climbs from one star to five, and work through ten story missions with combat, chases and paydays. Pick your weapon from the wheel — pistol, SMG or shotgun — tune your ride with engine, armor and paint upgrades at Pay 'n Spray, win street races against AI rivals, stock up at the city's shops, and climb the respect ladder from Street to King of Solmar across seven named districts. At three stars the police throw roadblocks across your road, and at four they lay spike strips. A HUD with a live minimap keeps you oriented, and you can save your progress and tune the radio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

That serves the game on http://localhost:8080 — open it in a desktop browser and play with keyboard and mouse. No build step, no network: everything the game needs ships under `game/`.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — move / drive
- **Shift** — sprint
- **F** — enter / exit a car
- **E** — interact (garage, shops, street race)
- **1 2 3 / Q** — weapon wheel: pistol, SMG, shotgun / cycle
- **Mouse** — aim and look, click to shoot
- **R** — change radio station
- **Esc** — pause (save from the pause menu)
