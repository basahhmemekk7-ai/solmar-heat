Solmar Heat is a 3D open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Roam a living 9×9-block city on foot or behind the wheel, weave through traffic, steal cars, dodge or outrun the police as your wanted level climbs from one star to five, and work through six story missions with combat, chases and paydays. A HUD with a live minimap keeps you oriented, and you can save your progress, tune the radio, and get your ride resprayed at Pay 'n Spray.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

That serves the game on http://localhost:8080 — open it in a desktop browser and play with keyboard and mouse. No build step, no network: everything the game needs ships under `game/`.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — move / drive
- **Shift** — sprint
- **F** — enter / exit a car
- **Mouse** — aim and look, click to shoot
- **Esc** — pause (save from the pause menu)
